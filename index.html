01 — About

I like figuring out how things work—and how they could work better.

My path to Information Systems started with curiosity. I began sitting in on a friend’s computer science lectures, then taught myself some coding fundamentals. That experience opened up a new area for me to explore: the way technology can support people and improve everyday processes.

At Maryland’s Smith School, I’m building on that interest through coursework and hands-on experience. I’ve worked on workflow automation, account onboarding, reporting, and community support. I enjoy the details, but I’m just as interested in the people who rely on a process working well.

Currently learning more about Python, workflow automation, and cloud technology.
